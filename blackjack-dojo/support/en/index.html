Hit or Stand?

Support

Hit or Stand? is an iPhone app for practicing blackjack basic strategy. It checks whether each decision you make follows the strategy and shows the situations you miss most. The chips aren't real, and no real money is used.

FAQ

How do I recover energy?

Each game you start uses 1 energy. You can have up to 5 energy, and 1 energy recovers every 30 minutes. You can also recover 1 energy right away by watching a video ad to the end (up to 3 times a day; the count resets when your device's date changes).

Ads won't play

Check your connection and try again a little later. If you didn't give consent in the ad consent message, ads may not be available. You can change your choices from Privacy Settings in the Privacy section of Settings (shown only in regions where the consent message appears).

How do I delete my play history?

Go to Learn Strategy → Play Analysis and tap Delete Play History. Deleted history can't be restored.

Why aren't some of my decisions evaluated?

The basic strategy chart doesn't support games with 1 or 2 decks, so decisions in those games aren't evaluated. To have your decisions checked, set the number of decks to 6 or 8 in Settings.
