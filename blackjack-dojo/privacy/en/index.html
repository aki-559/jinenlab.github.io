Hit or Stand?

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how JinenLab (“we”) handles information in the iPhone app “Hit or Stand?” (called “ブラックジャック道場” in Japanese; the “App”).

1. Information we collect

The App has no accounts or sign-in. We don't collect your name, email address, phone number, or any other information that identifies you through the App, and the App doesn't send your information to our servers.

If you contact us, we use your email address and message only to reply to you.

2. Information stored only on your device

The App stores the following information only on your device. It is not sent to us or to any third party.

  • Play history (your decisions in each hand and whether they were correct)
  • Your game rule settings
  • Your energy
  • How many ads you've watched today
  • Your ad consent choices (see “4. Consent management”)

Depending on your device settings, this information may be included in device backups, such as iCloud Backup.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App uses Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK), provided by Google LLC, to show video ads (rewarded ads) that recover energy. Ads are shown only when you choose to watch one.

To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect information such as:

  • IP address (which may be used to estimate your approximate location)
  • Device information, such as the device model, OS version, and language
  • An app-specific device identifier (Identifier for Vendor, or IDFV)
  • Ad views, taps, and other interactions with ads
  • Diagnostic information about how the App runs

The App doesn't ask for permission to track you (App Tracking Transparency), so the App never gives your Advertising Identifier (IDFA) to Google.

To learn how Google handles information, see:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • How Google uses advertising

4. Consent management

In the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK, Switzerland, and US states with privacy laws, the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for your consent about how ads use your data. To find out whether this message is needed, the App contacts Google when it starts.

In these regions, you can change your choices at any time from Privacy Settings in the Privacy section of the App's Settings. If you don't consent, you can still use all of the App's game and learning features (recovering energy with ads may be unavailable).

Residents of some US states may have rights under state law, such as the right to opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information for targeted advertising. You can make this choice in Privacy Settings as well. We don't sell your personal information ourselves.

5. Update checks

To let you know about new versions, the App downloads a settings file with the latest version number from GitHub Pages (a service of GitHub, Inc.) when it starts. The App doesn't send any of its information, such as your play history. However, GitHub may keep access logs, including your IP address. For details, see the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

6. Deleting your information

  • You can delete your play history with Delete Play History in Learn Strategy → Play Analysis in the App.
  • Deleting the App from your device deletes all of the information it stored on the device.
  • For information collected by Google, you can review or delete it as described in Google's Privacy Policy.

7. Age

The App is intended for people aged 18 and older. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. No real money

The App is for learning blackjack strategy. The chips and bankroll in the App are not real. You can't bet real money, win prizes, or cash out.

9. Changes to this policy

We may change this policy when the App's features or applicable laws change. If we do, we'll post the changes and the date on this page.

10. Contact us

If you have questions about this policy or how the App handles information, please email us.
